Face recognition models shipped with My Visuals
================================================

These files are loaded only when you scan or match faces. Photos are
processed in the browser. The models are not a remote identification service.

tiny_face_detector_model
face_landmark_68_tiny_model
  From @vladmandic/face-api 1.7.15 (MIT). See vendor/FACE-API-LICENSE.txt.
  The tiny face detector is a compact Tiny YOLOv2-style network trained for
  browser face detection.

face_recognition_model
  Quantized ResNet weights published with face-api.js. The architecture and
  original training are the dlib face-recognition model by Davis King
  (Boost Software License). It produces a 128-number signature used only to
  compare faces inside this library. It is not a database of identified people.

TensorFlow.js, which runs the models, is bundled inside vendor/face-api.js
and is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
